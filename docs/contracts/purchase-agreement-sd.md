# Residential Purchase Agreement (South Dakota) - DRAFT

Date: ____________

Seller: ______________________________ ("Seller")
Buyer: Deaven Mabry and/or assigns ("Buyer")

1. Property. Seller agrees to sell and Buyer agrees to buy the real property at ______________________________, Sioux Falls, SD _______, County of ______________, legal description: ______________________________________________, parcel ID ______________, with all fixtures (the "Property").

2. Price. $______________ total. Earnest money of $______ is due within 3 business days to ______________________ (title company) and is credited to the price at closing. Balance due at closing in certified funds.

3. Closing. On or before ____________, at the title company above. Seller delivers a warranty deed free of liens except those paid from proceeds. Possession at closing.

4. Inspection period. Buyer has ____ (10) days after acceptance to inspect the Property and cancel for any reason by written notice. If Buyer cancels within this period the earnest money is returned to Buyer in full.

5. Condition. Property is sold as is. Seller will not remove fixtures and will deliver the Property in its present condition, free of debris and personal property not included.

6. Title. Seller provides a title commitment within 10 days. Buyer has 5 days to object. If defects cannot be cured by closing, Buyer may cancel and receive the earnest money.

7. Costs and proration. Taxes prorated to closing. Seller pays owner's title policy and deed preparation; Buyer pays recording fees. Each pays own attorney.

8. Assignment. Buyer may assign this agreement to a third party without Seller's consent. Buyer will notify Seller in writing of any assignment before closing. Seller acknowledges Buyer is an investor who may assign this contract or resell the Property for a profit.

9. Default. If Buyer defaults after the inspection period, Seller keeps the earnest money as sole remedy. If Seller defaults, Buyer may recover the earnest money or seek specific performance.

10. Disclosures. Seller will complete the South Dakota Seller's Property Condition Disclosure Statement (SDCL 43-4-38) unless exempt. Lead paint disclosure attached for homes built before 1978.

11. Entire agreement. This is the whole agreement. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. Governed by South Dakota law.
