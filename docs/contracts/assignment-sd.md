2. Assignment. Assignor assigns all rights and obligations under the Contract to Assignee. Assignee agrees to perform all Buyer obligations, including closing on or before the closing date.

3. Assignment fee. Assignee pays Assignor an assignment fee of $______________, of which $______ is a non-refundable deposit paid on signing this Assignment to ______________________ (title company), and the balance is paid to Assignor through the title company at closing.

5. As is. Assignee has inspected the property and the Contract and takes both as is. Assignor makes no warranty about the property, title, or Seller's performance.

6. Default. If Assignee fails to close, Assignee forfeits the deposit in section 3 and Assignor may close itself or reassign. If the Seller fails to close for reasons outside Assignee's control, the deposit is returned to Assignee.
