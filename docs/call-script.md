# Seller call script (Sioux Falls)

Goal of the first call: find out if they would sell, not to make an offer.

Open
"Hi, is this [name]? My name is Deaven, I'm a local investor here in Sioux Falls. I'm calling about the house at [address]. Do you still own it?"

If yes
"I buy a few houses a year in town, as is, cash, and I close on your timeline. Have you thought about selling it?"

Listen. Then ask, in this order:
1. What's the situation with the house right now? (rented, vacant, family, behind on taxes)
2. If you sold, when would you want to close?
3. Is there a mortgage on it? Roughly what's owed?
4. What condition is it in? Roof, furnace, water heater, anything you'd fix before selling?
5. Do you have a number in mind?

Close
"Here's how I work: I come by, walk it in 15 minutes, and give you a written cash offer the same day. No repairs, no cleaning, no fees. When works, this week or next?"

If no
"No problem. If that ever changes, I'll be the easy call. Mind if I check back in a couple of months?" Log it and set a 60-day follow-up.

Rules
- Never say you'll close with your own cash if you plan to assign. Say "my partners and I" or "we".
- Never quote a price on the first call.
- Pre-foreclosure owners: lead with "I know the sale date is [date]; there may still be options" and offer to walk through them. Do not promise to stop the sale.
- Do not text or robocall owners. Calls you dial yourself, letters, and door knocks only.
- Log every call in the app, including no answers. Most deals come from call 4 to 8.
