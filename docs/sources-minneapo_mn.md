# Minneapolis (Hennepin County) sources

Working, free, unattended:
- Hennepin County parcels: `gis.hennepin.us/arcgis/rest/services/HennepinData/LAND_PROPERTY/MapServer/1` (448k; `PID` 13 digits, situs as `HOUSE_NO` + `FRAC_HOUSE_NO` + `STREET_NM`, `OWNER_NM`, taxpayer mailing block in `TAXPAYER_NM_1..3`, `PR_TYP_NM1` property type, `HMSTD_CD1` homestead H/N, `BUILD_YR`, `MKT_VAL_TOT`, `SALE_DATE` as `YYYYMM`, `SALE_PRICE`, `SALE_CODE_NAME`, `EARLIEST_DELQ_YR` two digit year of the oldest unpaid tax, `COMP_JUDG_IND` T when a tax judgment is entered, `FORFEIT_LAND_IND`). Residential is `PR_TYP_NM1 IN ('RESIDENTIAL','RESIDENTIAL-TWO UNIT','TOWNHOUSE','RESIDENTIAL-ZERO LOT LINE')` (331k; condos 47k left out). Pull is non-homestead or delinquent or judgment (48.7k, about 1 minute), plus every parcel with a city case fetched by id (55k total).
- Derived from the parcel record: delinquent taxes (`EARLIEST_DELQ_YR` not blank, 1.7k residential), tax judgment (`COMP_JUDG_IND = 'T'`, 371 residential). A judgment means the county has moved the unpaid taxes to court; forfeiture follows after the redemption period.
- City of Minneapolis inspections `services.arcgis.com/afSMGVsC7QlRK1kZ/arcgis/rest/services/CaseViolations/FeatureServer/0` (386k violation rows since 2016, updated daily, `APN` matches the county `PID`, `Display` is the address). Open = `Resolved_Date IS NULL`. Used four ways: boarded or unsafe (`Violation_Code_Description` in Police Authorized Boarding, Property Found Boarded, Unsafe Buildings, Vacant Buildings; 1.9k rows), vacant building registration (`Case_Type = 'VBR'`, 844 rows), housing cases (`Case_Group = 'HISINSP'` with status Enforce/Assessed/Pending, 9.1k rows; rental inspections `HISRENTAL` are routine and skipped), nuisance (`Case_Type = 'Nuisance'`, 18.6k rows). 15.7k distinct parcels carry an open case.

Gotchas:
- The taxpayer mailing block is three free text lines. Usually line 1 is the street and line 2 is `CITY ST ZIP`, but for about 29% of homestead parcels (9% of the delinquent ones) line 1 is a second owner name and the street is on line 2. Those few owner-occupied houses show a name as `mail` and get the absentee flag by mistake. `mail_city` holds `CITY ST ZIP`, so `mail_state` is blank and "out of state" never fires here. Server side the absentee filter is the homestead flag, which is right regardless.
- Situs uses a trailing directional (`26TH ST E`) while the taxpayer line often uses a leading one (`E 26TH ST`), so about 8 of the 1,838 absentee hot leads are the owner's own house. Comparing sorted tokens in the scorer would fix it.
- `SALE_DATE` is `YYYYMM`, which `parcels.sale_date` does not parse, so sale dates are blank and the cash buyer list is empty until that helper accepts a 6 digit month. `SALE_CODE_NAME` has PROBATE DEED (1.9k) and QUIT CLAIM DEED (1.8k) but no usable date for a since window, so they are not scored yet.
- Fields are space padded; compare with `<> '  '` (two spaces) for `EARLIEST_DELQ_YR`.

Dead ends: Minneapolis `Condemned_by_Boarding` and `VBR_and_Vacant_CPED_Properties` layers are 2016 snapshots, superseded by CaseViolations; `Week_of_Inspections` has no query support. Hennepin sheriff mortgage foreclosure sales live at `foreclosure.hennepin.us` (an interactive app, no open layer found yet). Hennepin tax forfeited land list is a PDF/auction page.

Next: `CaseInspections` layer (380k, same org) for failed inspections; sheriff foreclosure sale notices (Minnesota forecloses by advertisement, six month redemption); St. Paul (Ramsey County) parcels and its vacant building list; make `sale_date` accept `YYYYMM` so Hennepin cash buyers appear.
